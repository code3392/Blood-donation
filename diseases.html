• O− (O নেগেটিভ): সর্বজনীন দাতা (Universal Donor) — লোহিত রক্তকণিকায় কোনো A, B বা Rh অ্যান্টিজেন না থাকায় যে কোনো গ্রুপকে রক্ত দিতে পারে।

• Rh নিয়ম: Rh-পজিটিভ রক্ত কখনও Rh-নেগেটিভ রোগীকে দেওয়া যাবে না। Rh-নেগেটিভ রোগীকে অবশ্যই Rh-নেগেটিভ রক্ত দিতে হবে।

Genetic Disorder Frequent Transfusion

Thalassemia (থ্যালাসেমিয়া)

An inherited genetic condition causing defective hemoglobin production. Patients with Thalassemia Major cannot produce sufficient healthy red blood cells, leading to severe chronic anemia, bone deformities, and organ enlargement.

Transfusion Cycle:Every 2 to 4 weeks
Product Required:Leukoreduced Packed RBCs (PRBC)
Key Precaution:Iron Chelation Therapy (Desferal / Kelfer)
Compatibility:Extended Rh (C, c, E, e) & Kell Phenotyping

Doctor’s Guidance: Always provide leucodepleted red cells to prevent alloimmunization and febrile reactions. Monitor serum ferritin routinely to prevent cardiac and liver iron toxicity.

Request Blood for Thalassemia
Bone Marrow / Blood Loss Urgent / Planned

Severe & Aplastic Anemia (রক্তস্বল্পতা)

Aplastic anemia is bone marrow failure where stem cells fail to produce RBCs, WBCs, and platelets. Severe nutritional or hemolytic anemia causes dangerously low hemoglobin (< 6-7 g/dL) leading to heart strain.

Transfusion Threshold:Hb < 7.0 g/dL (or symptomatic)
Product Required:Packed Red Blood Cells (PRBC)
Critical Risk:Volume overload & alloimmunization

Doctor’s Guidance: Do not transfuse whole blood if iron-deficiency can be corrected with IV iron. In aplastic anemia, blood must be irradiated to prevent graft-versus-host disease (TA-GVHD).

Request Blood for Anemia
Hematologic Cancer Emergency Critical

Leukemia & Blood Cancers (লিউকেমিয়া)

Malignancies of blood-forming tissue (ALL, AML, Lymphoma). Chemotherapy and marrow infiltration cause profound pancytopenia — life-threatening drops in both red cells and clotting platelets.

Transfusion Need:Single Donor Platelets (SDP) & PRBC
Platelet Trigger:Count < 10,000/µL or active bleeding
Special Processing:Irradiated & Leukodepleted Products

Doctor’s Guidance: Platelet transfusions are time-sensitive. Coordinate early with apheresis donors who have not taken aspirin or NSAIDs in the past 72 hours.

Request Platelets / Blood
Coagulation Disorder Clotting Emergency

Hemophilia & Bleeding Disorders (হিমোফিলিয়া)

Deficiency of clotting Factor VIII (Hemophilia A) or Factor IX (Hemophilia B). Minor trauma can cause devastating internal joint, muscle, or intracranial bleeds.

Primary Treatment:Factor Concentrates (Recombinant/Plasma)
Alternative Emergency:Fresh Frozen Plasma (FFP) / Cryo
Avoid:Whole blood (insufficient factor concentration)

Doctor’s Guidance: Standard whole blood is ineffective for hemophilia bleeds. Contact the hematology center urgently for factor vials or specialized Cryoprecipitate.

Seasonal Epidemic Critical Window

Dengue with Thrombocytopenia (ডেঙ্গু)

Dengue hemorrhagic fever causes immune-mediated destruction of platelets and capillary plasma leakage. While platelet count drops sharply, prophylactic transfusion is usually NOT necessary unless severe bleeding exists.

Transfusion Criteria:Active bleeding OR count < 10,000/µL
Primary Therapy:Judicious IV crystalloid fluid management
Blood Products:Platelet Concentrates / Fresh Whole Blood

Doctor’s Guidance: Avoid panicking over numerical platelet counts. Fluid resuscitation is paramount. Follow the National Guidelines for Clinical Management of Dengue Syndrome.

Request Urgent Platelets
Hemoglobinopathy / Surgery Urgent Response

Sickle Cell Disease & Emergency Trauma (সিকেল সেল ও ট্রমা)

Sickle cell patients experience acute chest syndrome or splenic sequestration. In road traffic accidents and post-partum hemorrhage, massive blood loss requires immediate, synchronized transfusion.

Sickle Cell:Exchange transfusion to reduce HbS < 30%
Trauma / MTP:1:1:1 ratio (PRBC, FFP, Platelets)
Universal Blood:O-Negative used prior to crossmatch

Doctor’s Guidance: In massive hemorrhage, warm blood products to prevent hypothermia and coagulopathy. Always activate hospital emergency transfusion protocols immediately.
