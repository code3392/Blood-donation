HOW LIFELINE WORKS

Designed around the moments that matter.

From the second an emergency occurs until blood is delivered to the hospital bedside, Lifeline streamlines the entire coordination chain without confusion.

01
🔍

1. Search or Post Request

Search willing donors across cities and countries worldwide or publish an urgent request with blood group, required units, hospital details, and condition.

02
🔬

2. Scientific Auto-Matching

Our algorithm instantly executes scientific transfusion rules to find compatible donor groups (e.g. O- universal, Rh compatibility) nearby.

03
💬

3. WhatsApp Coordination

Open direct encrypted chat with matching donors. Attach medical cross-match prescriptions and hospital location slips instantly.

04
🩸

4. Safe Hospital Donation

Donor arrives at hospital blood bank for pre-screening, doctor verification, and safe transfusion under WHO and international safe blood guidelines.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Everything you need to know

Who can donate blood?

Healthy adults aged 18–60 weighing at least 45 kg (females) or 50 kg (males), with a minimum 3–4 month gap since their last whole blood donation.

How does scientific donor matching work?

Lifeline follows international transfusion compatibility standards. It verifies antigen-antibody compatibility between donor RBCs and recipient plasma.

Can I attach medical documents?

Yes! In the WhatsApp messaging modal or dashboard chat, you can click the paperclip icon (📎) to attach doctor prescriptions and test reports.
