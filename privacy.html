DATA PROTECTION & CONFIDENTIALITY

Privacy Policy

Last revised: October 2026 • Built on Consent-First Principles

1. Our Privacy Pledge

At Lifeline, we understand that donating or seeking blood involves sensitive personal and medical circumstances. We are fundamentally committed to data minimization, transparency, and donor privacy. We do not sell, rent, monetize, or broker personal data to advertising companies or data brokers.

2. Information We Collect & Why

  • Account Authentication: Email and password (encrypted and salted using Supabase Authentication with industry-standard bcrypt/Argon2 hashing).
  • Volunteer Donor Profile: Name, blood group, district, optional neighborhood/upazila, contact phone number, and last donation date. This information is provided voluntarily to connect you with emergency patients.
  • Emergency Blood Requests: Patient/contact name, blood group requested, hospital/district location, units needed, urgency level, and optional condition details.
  • Direct WhatsApp-Style Messaging: Text and file attachments (prescriptions, hospital admission slips, cross-match reports) sent between logged-in users are stored securely and isolated with database Row-Level Security (RLS).
  • Approximate Location Data: If you use the "Detect my location" feature, the browser detects approximate coordinates strictly to identify your district or nearby area. Exact GPS coordinates are never displayed on public cards or revealed to third parties.

3. How We Protect Donor Information

Unlike open social media groups where phone numbers are scraped by commercial telemarketers, Lifeline enforces strict protective layers:

  • Protected Directory: Anonymous visitors cannot view donor phone numbers or send direct messages. Users must sign in to access donor contact details.
  • Granular Availability Control: Donors can toggle their availability status to "Unavailable" with one click in the Dashboard whenever they are sick, travelling, or recently donated.
  • Row-Level Security (RLS): Supabase PostgreSQL RLS policies restrict message access so only the sender and intended recipient can view chat history and uploaded medical attachments.

4. Cookies & Local Storage

Lifeline uses cookies and browser local storage for:

  • Essential Security & Session Tokens: Keeping your account securely signed in.
  • Functional Preferences: Remembering your language preference (Bangla vs English) and cookie consent state.
  • No Third-Party Advertising Cookies: We do not deploy behavioral ad trackers or cross-site tracking cookies.

5. Your Rights: Access, Correction & Deletion

You retain complete sovereignty over your data:

  • You can edit your contact information, name, and blood group anytime in your Donor Dashboard.
  • You can withdraw your consent to appear in the directory at any time.
  • You can permanently delete your blood requests or request full account deactivation by contacting support or deleting your profile in the Dashboard.

6. Security Infrastructure

Lifeline utilizes 256-bit Transport Layer Security (TLS/SSL) encryption for all traffic in transit, cloud-native PostgreSQL database security, and role-based access management.
