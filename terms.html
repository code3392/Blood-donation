LEGAL AGREEMENT

Terms and Conditions

Last revised: October 2026 • Effective for all Lifeline users in Bangladesh

1. Nature of the Service & Humanitarian Mission

Lifeline is an independent, non-profit humanitarian emergency donor matching platform designed to facilitate communication between voluntary blood donors and families/hospitals in urgent need of blood across Bangladesh. By accessing or registering on Lifeline, you agree to comply with and be bound by these Terms and Conditions.

2. Strict Voluntary, Non-Commercial Blood Donation

In accordance with the Safe Blood Transfusion Act, 2002 of Bangladesh and international World Health Organization (WHO) humanitarian protocols:

  • Blood donation facilitated through Lifeline is 100% voluntary and unpaid.
  • No donor, recipient, intermediary, or third party is permitted to offer, request, demand, or accept money, commercial remuneration, or material goods in exchange for blood or blood products.
  • Any user found attempting commercial blood sales or brokerage will be permanently banned immediately and reported to relevant healthcare law enforcement authorities.

3. Medical Disclaimer & Blood Center Responsibility

Lifeline is a digital communication and matching facilitator. Lifeline is NOT a hospital, blood bank, testing laboratory, medical practitioner, or clinical healthcare provider:

  • Lifeline does not draw, store, test, cross-match, or transfuse blood.
  • Hospital Blood Bank Authority: The treating hospital’s registered blood bank or transfusion medicine department is exclusively responsible for screening donors, testing for Transfusion-Transmitted Infections (TTIs including HIV, Hepatitis B, Hepatitis C, Syphilis, and Malaria), performing mandatory ABO and Rh cross-matching, and approving donor fitness.
  • In life-threatening situations, seekers must immediately contact emergency medical services and physical blood centers directly without solely relying on digital responses.

4. User Accounts & Protected Network Privacy

Lifeline enforces protected access controls to protect voluntary donors from unsolicited spam, marketing, and harassment:

  • Users must sign in with a valid account to view donor contact details and communicate via WhatsApp-style messaging.
  • Users agree never to scrape, copy, redistribute, harvest, or publish personal phone numbers or contact data obtained from the Lifeline directory.
  • Users agree never to share OTPs, passwords, or personal financial details in message communications.

5. Request Publication & Integrity

Requesters agree to post only authentic, verified blood needs with correct patient name, hospital location, and accurate units needed. Once the blood requirement is satisfied, the requester agrees to promptly mark the request as "Fulfilled" or "Cancelled" via the Donor Dashboard to prevent unnecessary donor mobilization.

6. Termination of Access

Lifeline reserves the right to suspend, deactivate, or delete any account or request that violates these terms, engages in spam, provides fraudulent information, or threatens the safety and well-being of the volunteer donor community.

7. Governing Law

These terms shall be governed by and interpreted under the laws of the People's Republic of Bangladesh.
